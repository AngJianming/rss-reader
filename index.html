Where is it Used? Database contexts (like DbContext) Business logic services that are per-request Real-life Example: Where to be careful with this? Code: services.AddScoped(); 3) Transient: New Every Time A Transient service is created every time it's requested. Even within the same request, asking twice gives you two different instances.
