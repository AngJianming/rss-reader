This is the simplest and most powerful move. ·---· · · | | · · · · | 0 | · · · · | | ·---· · · Every edge around the 0 is guaranteed to have no line. A 3 in any corner of the grid forces two edges along the grid border. The corner vertex must have exactly 2 lines passing through, and with 3 required edges, two must be the border edges. ·---·---· | 3 | · · · | · · · Two 3s next to each other horizontally or vertically create an S-shaped or Z-shaped line pattern. They share a middle edge, with alternating top/bottom (or left/right) edges. ·---· · | 3 | · ·---· | 3 | · ·---· Every vertex (dot) in the puzzle must have either 0 or exactly 2 lines passing through it. If a vertex already has 2 lines, all other edges at that vertex are definitely "no line." If a vertex has 1 line and only one other possible edge, that edge must also be a line. The solution must be a single closed loop. If drawing a line would create a small closed loop disconnected from the rest, that line is impossible.
