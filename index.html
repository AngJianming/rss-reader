Instead of treating language as something to decode, it treats it as something to use. You’re not just passively consuming translations, you’re engaging with patterns, context, and recall in a more active loop. What stood out to me is how quickly it shifts you out of “study mode” and into “usage mode.” It feels closer to building intuition than studying rules. The interesting contrast What I didn’t expect is how well these two complement each other: Linguaboard → helps you understand nuance and meaning One is about interpretation, the other about retention through interaction. Put together, they highlight something a lot of dev tools miss: Language work isn’t one problem. It’s at least two: understanding, and using.
