Photorealistic output requires models to get lighting, shadows, skin texture, reflections and perspective consistent at once. Small errors such as extra fingers, warped text, or mismatched light sources instantly break the illusion. Modern diffusion models handle this far better than earlier GANs because they iteratively denoise an image while conditioning on the prompt. Describe the subject first, then the setting, then the style. Name the camera and lens ("35mm, f/1.8, shallow depth of field") to nudge realistic optics. Specify lighting ("golden hour", "softbox studio light", "overcast"). Use negative prompts to remove artifacts like blur, watermarks, or distorted hands. Iterate: change one variable at a time so you learn what each word does. Start at low resolution to explore compositions quickly. Pick the best candidate and upscale it. Use inpainting to fix local problems instead of regenerating everything. Keep a prompt log so good results are reproducible. There are many browser-based generators today.
