What was happening? The database became the bottleneck. Too many reads. Too many writes. Too many queries happening at the same time. But users start feeling: • slow responses • delays • timeouts And performance keeps getting worse as you grow. The solution You don’t remove the database. You reduce how much you depend on it. Mental model Think of the database like a single cashier. At first, no line. Then more people arrive. Now everyone is waiting…Even though the cashier is working perfectly.
