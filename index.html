Most modern browsers support the Gamepad API, so your controller should be detected instantly. To check for stick drift, watch the thumbstick readouts while your hands are completely off the controller. The values should sit at zero. If they wander or jitter, your stick is drifting. A healthy stick returns to exactly zero and moves smoothly through its full range when you rotate it slowly. For buttons and triggers, press each one individually and confirm the on-screen indicator lights up once per press. Watch out for buttons that trigger without being pressed, or fail to register at all — both are signs of a worn switch. Test the D-pad and shoulder buttons the same way. Finally, check the deadzone: tiny movements at the edges of the stick's range should read near zero on a well-calibrated controller.
