I opened the PE header, flipped the bits that pin it to a platform back to AnyCPU, and it loaded. That became pefix fix. The header turned out to be the easy case. The failures that actually bite are about the folder around the DLL: a dependency that never got copied, two copies of one assembly at different versions, a method a plugin calls that the shipped library no longer has. None of it shows up until something loads the assembly at runtime. So pefix grew into a static loadability preflight over a whole folder. It reads each assembly's metadata (no Assembly.Load) and resolves every reference, type, member, field and interface against what's present.
