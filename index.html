Use a border when the edge of the image blends into the page. This often happens with screenshots that have a white or very light background. A thin gray or muted color border is usually enough. Thick borders can work for social graphics, but they often look heavy in documentation or product pages. Use transparent background when you want the image to sit naturally on different page colors.
